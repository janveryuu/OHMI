## Implementation

1. Isolate on `codex/ohmi-cinematic-review`; do not push to production branch or deploy.
2. Keep native HTML/CSS/JS. Introduce canonical `website/src` and a deterministic copy-and-verify build; stop treating checked-in dist as source.
3. Self-host Grandstander 900 and Inter with OFL texts. Preserve source illustration PNG and canonical mascot bytes.
4. Draw the single supplied image through a small native WebGL shader. Restrict independently phased wind to grass, hair, clothing, clouds and antenna tips. Preserve face/body landmarks. No generated video is available; do not pretend this is an image-to-video loop.
5. Use WAAPI entrance, 160 ms headline stagger, 8px card lift and 1.05 image scale. Provide pause, reduced motion, hidden/offscreen pause and still fallback. No framework or runtime dependencies.
6. Keep existing anchor destinations, add feature anchors and mobile navigation. Use actual local PWA screenshots labeled frontend previews, without live AI claims.
7. Validate build; browser checks at desktop/tablet/mobile, keyboard, reduced motion, no JS/WebGL, motion regions, pause/resume and links. Capture screenshots and a short recording. Review and record limitations before owner review.
